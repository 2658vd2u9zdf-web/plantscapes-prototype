Planting decisions, with the site in view

Design for the place that is already there.

Plantscapes helps landscape designers define a project, outline a site and test a planting workflow. This prototype uses clearly labelled synthetic ecology data to explore the experience; it is not a source of planting advice.

What it does

Moves from intent to an outlined plot, suggested conditions, a reviewable demo palette and export.

What it does not do yet

It does not replace surveys or generate a construction-ready planting plan. The spatial workspace is a sketching scaffold.

Where it works

You can test a site anywhere in the Netherlands. Its plant-fit scores and site profiles are invented fixtures awaiting reviewed data.

Test an arrangement.

Draw planting zones to create coded hatch masses, individual tree and accent symbols, and a species quantity key.

Simulation
Illustrative mockup, not a planting specification.Dense non-tree planting is shown as species-coded hatch masses to keep the plan readable. Each circle remains one individual tree or accent; quantities include all simulated plants. Density uses the area measured from your map-drawn zones. Open ground, water and 1.5 m-wide paths are excluded. A 1 m radius measured from each tree trunk is exempt from the non-tree minimum; adjust the test buffer below. Plant traits, spacing and local suitability remain unverified.
